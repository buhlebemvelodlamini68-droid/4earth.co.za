About Us

4Earth is a youth-led climate and food security initiative based in South Africa. We equip high school learners with climate literacy, sustainable agriculture skills, and leadership development to drive measurable community impact.

Our Programs

  • Climate Change Education & Workshops
  • Youth Climate Leadership Development
  • School Food Growing & Greenhouse Revitalisation
  • Community-Based Sustainable Agriculture

Our Impact Model

40% of produce supports school nutrition programs in disadvantaged communities. 60% sustains the initiative and enables growth.
